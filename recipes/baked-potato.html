Baked Potato

Description

Its a baked potato what do you really need to know? Its starch, alot of fats, alot of salt garnish with whatever you like, crispy bacon bits, slather it in a can of baked beans. Skies the limit.

Ingredients

  • a potato (preferrably a russet)
  • oil (something with a high smoke point)
  • salt
  • butter
  • (optional) can of baked beans, green onion (for garnish), bacon bits, etc.

Steps

  1. Wash and then dry the potato
  2. rub oil over the skin
  3. put salt on the skin
  4. Poke holes in the surface all over
  5. put in the microwave on high for 5 minutes
  6. turn over
  7. put it on for another 5 minutes
  8. remove from microwave and let cool 2 minutes
  9. (I like to mash up the interior with a fork first) slice it open and add a knob of butter
  10. garnish as desired and serve
